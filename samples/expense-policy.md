# Expense policy

## Approvals

Expenses up to 500 dollars are approved by your line manager.
Expenses over 500 dollars must also be approved by the finance director.

## Travel

Book economy class for flights under six hours. Hotel spend is capped at 200 dollars per night
in most cities and 300 dollars per night in London, New York and San Francisco.

## Reimbursement

Submit receipts in the expenses tool within 30 days. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run.
