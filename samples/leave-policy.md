# Leave policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per year, plus public holidays.
Unused leave can be carried over into the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.
Requests longer than two weeks need manager approval at least one month in advance.

| Tenure | Extra annual leave days |
|---|---|
| 5+ years | 2 |
| 10+ years | 5 |

## Sick leave

Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required
for absences longer than three consecutive working days.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within the first year.
