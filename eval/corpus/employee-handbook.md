# Halcyon Robotics Employee Handbook

This handbook applies to all Halcyon Robotics employees. Where a local law gives you more generous terms, the local law applies.

## Working hours

Halcyon uses flexible working hours. Everyone is expected to be available during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 in their local time zone. Outside core hours you can arrange your day with your team.

## Remote and hybrid work

Office-assigned employees work hybrid and are expected in the office two days a week, on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Fully remote roles must be approved by a VP before an offer is made.

You may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year. Requests to work from abroad need HR approval and must be submitted at least two weeks before you travel.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata allowance.

You can carry over up to 5 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, after which they expire.

Public holidays follow the calendar of the office you are assigned to. Remote employees follow the public holidays of the country they are employed in.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 12 paid sick days per calendar year. A doctor's note is required if you are off sick for more than four consecutive working days.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver of a new child, by birth or adoption, receives 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Bereavement leave

You can take 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member, and 2 days for an extended family member.

## Volunteering

Every employee gets 2 paid volunteering days per year to support a registered charity of their choice.

## Probation and notice periods

New employees have a probation period of 90 days. During probation the notice period is one week on either side.

After probation, the notice period is one month for individual contributors and three months for people managers.

## Code of conduct

Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

Employees may accept gifts or hospitality from vendors only up to a value of 75 dollars, and every gift must be recorded in the gift register. Cash gifts are never allowed.

## Reporting concerns

If you see something that breaks this handbook or the law, tell your manager, HR, or the ethics team at ethics@halcyon.example. You can also report anonymously through the EthicsPoint hotline. Halcyon does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
