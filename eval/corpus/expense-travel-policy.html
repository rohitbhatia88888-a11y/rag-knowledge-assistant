Expense and Travel Policy

This policy explains what Halcyon Robotics reimburses and how to claim it. Spend company money as carefully as you would your own.

Approvals

Every expense claim needs approval before it is reimbursed. The approvers depend on the total amount of the claim.

Claim amountRequired approvers
Up to $500Line manager
$500.01 to $5,000Line manager and department head
Over $5,000Line manager, department head and CFO

Submitting claims

Submit every claim with an itemised receipt in Ledgerly within 30 days of the expense. Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run. Payroll runs twice a month, on the 15th and on the last business day of the month.

Flights

Book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class always requires VP approval in advance.

Hotels

Hotel costs are capped per night, excluding taxes. If you stay above the cap, the difference is not reimbursed.

Meals and mileage

While travelling, meals are covered by a per diem of $75 per day for domestic US trips and $90 per day for international trips. Personal car use for business travel is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile.

Client entertainment

Client entertainment is capped at $150 per person. Alcohol is reimbursed only as part of client entertainment, up to $50 per person.

Corporate cards

Employees who travel for work more than four times a year can request a corporate card. Corporate cards have a monthly limit of $10,000.

Not reimbursable

The following are never reimbursed: alcohol outside client entertainment, traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment, hotel minibar charges, and gym memberships. Gym memberships are covered by the wellness stipend instead.
