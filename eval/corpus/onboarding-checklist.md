# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

## Day one

- Collect your laptop from IT and enroll it in mobile device management (MDM).
- Set up your 1Password account. Engineers also set up their YubiKey.
- Join the #welcome and #announcements Slack channels.

## First week

- Complete security awareness training. It must be finished within your first 7 days.
- Meet your onboarding buddy, who will check in with you weekly for your first month.
- Submit your payroll and tax forms to People Operations.

## First month

- Enroll in benefits within 30 days of your start date. If you miss this window you must wait for open enrollment, unless you have a qualifying life event.
- Agree on your 30-60-90 day goals with your manager.

## Equipment

Remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 750 dollars for a desk, chair or other equipment. Order monitors, keyboards and other peripherals through the IT portal.

## First 90 days

Your probation review with your manager happens at the end of your first 90 days.
