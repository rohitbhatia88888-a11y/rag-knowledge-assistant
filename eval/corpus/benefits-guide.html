Halcyon Robotics Benefits Guide

This guide summarises the benefits available to US employees of Halcyon Robotics for the current plan year. Plan documents from each provider take precedence if anything here differs.

Health insurance

Medical coverage is provided by BlueHarbor Health. You can choose one of three plans. Premiums below are what you pay per month; Halcyon pays the rest.

If you choose the Bronze HSA plan, Halcyon contributes $1,000 per year to your health savings account for employee-only coverage, or $2,000 per year for family coverage.

Dental and vision

Dental insurance is provided by BrightSmile Dental and covers two cleanings per year at no cost. Orthodontics are covered at 50 percent up to a lifetime maximum of $2,000.

Vision insurance is provided by ClearView Vision. It includes a yearly eye exam and a $150 allowance for frames or contact lenses every 24 months.

Retirement: 401(k)

New employees are automatically enrolled in the 401(k) plan at a 3 percent contribution, which you can change at any time.

Halcyon matches 100 percent of the first 4 percent of salary you contribute, plus 50 percent of the next 2 percent. The company match vests over three years, one third at each work anniversary.

Life and disability insurance

Halcyon provides life insurance equal to two times your annual base salary at no cost. Short-term disability pays 70 percent of base salary for up to 26 weeks.

Wellness stipend

Every employee receives a wellness stipend of $600 per year. It can be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, sports equipment and meditation or mental health apps. Claim it through Ledgerly under the Wellness category.

Learning and development

Each employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per year for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Any single purchase above $500 needs your manager's approval before you buy it.

Commuter benefit

You can set aside up to $300 per month before tax for public transit and parking costs.

Open enrollment and life events

Open enrollment runs from November 1 to November 15 each year, and your choices take effect on January 1. Outside open enrollment you can only change plans after a qualifying life event, such as marriage or the birth of a child, and you must make the change within 30 days of the event.
