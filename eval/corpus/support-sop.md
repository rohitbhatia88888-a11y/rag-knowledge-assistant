# Customer Support Standard Operating Procedure

## Service levels by customer tier

| Customer tier | First response | Resolution target | Channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | 1 hour, 24/7 | 1 business day | Phone, email, Slack Connect |
| Business | 4 business hours | 3 business days | Email, chat |
| Starter | 1 business day | 5 business days | Email |

## Refunds

Support agents can issue refunds of up to 1,000 dollars without approval. Refunds between 1,000 and 10,000 dollars need approval from a support manager. Refunds above 10,000 dollars must be approved by the VP of Finance.

## Escalating to engineering

Escalate bugs to engineering through the ESC project in Jira. Every escalation must include the customer tier and clear reproduction steps. Engineering assigns a severity using the on-call runbook.

## Tools and quality

Use the shared macros in Zendesk for common answers. The team's customer satisfaction (CSAT) target is 92 percent.

## Data deletion requests

Customer requests to delete their account data must be completed within 30 days. Forward every deletion request to privacy@halcyon.example the same day it arrives.
