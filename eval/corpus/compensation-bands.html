Engineering Compensation Bands

Confidential: HR and leadership only. These bands apply to the engineering job family for the current year.

Location tiers

Base salary is adjusted by location. Tier 1 (San Francisco and New York) is paid at 100 percent of the band. Tier 2 (Seattle, Boston and London) is paid at 92 percent. Tier 3, all other locations, is paid at 85 percent.

Bonus

The annual bonus target is 10 percent of base salary for levels L3 to L5 and 15 percent for L6 and L7. Bonuses are paid in March.

Merit increases and promotions

Annual merit increases take effect on April 1. This year's merit budget is 4 percent of payroll.

Promotions are decided twice a year, in April and October. A promotion usually comes with a base salary increase of 8 to 12 percent.

Off-cycle salary adjustments need approval from both the VP of the department and the HR business partner.
